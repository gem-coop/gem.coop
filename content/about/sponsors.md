## The Gem Cooperative

[The Gem Cooperative](https://github.com/gem-coop/governance) is the team of worker-owners that created, develops, maintains, and operates the gem.coop service.

## Fastly

[Fastly](https://fastly.com) is a CDN and edge compute provider. They generously sponsor bandwidth, storage, and edge compute for the gem.coop service through their [Fast Forward](https://fastly.com/fast-forward) program to support open source projects.

## Fly.io

[Fly.io](https://fly.io) is a cloud hosting service. They generously sponsor the servers that run [Peak](https://github.com/gem-coop/peak), the open source software powering the gem.coop service.

## Spinel Cooperative Corporation

[Spinel Cooperative](https://spinel.coop) is a Ruby open-source consultancy founded by former members of the Rails, RubyGems, and Bundler core teams. They acted as the initial legal entity backing the gem.coop service.

Their business is high-expertise consulting and retainers to resolve outages, fix project delays, wrangle test suites run amok, and much more, with a focus on web development in Ruby. Spinel also develops [`rv`](https://rv.dev), the fast Ruby version and gem management tool, with built-in support for the gem.coop service.

## Donated services

- [Honeybadger](https://honeybadger.io) provides error and exception tracking.
- [DNSimple](https://dnsimple.com) provides domain name resolution.
- [Honeycomb](https://honeycomb.io) provides observability and metrics.
- [GitHub](https://github.com) provides repository hosting, CI runners.
