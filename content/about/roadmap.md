## Complete

* establish public governance, inviting all rubygems.org contributors within the last year
* make gem.coop a public mirror of rubygems.org
* beta testing for dependency cooldowns, for increased security
* beta testing for namespaces, allowing unlimited gem publishing
* complete and publish a security policy & terms of service
* general availability for namespaces, now at gem.coop/sign_up

## In progress

* final privacy policy
* general availability for cooldowns
* supporter subscriptions, for a sustainable service

## Future plans

* free accounts, for increased rate limits
* automatic pull request preview gems, for easy testing
* precompiled native extensions, for security and speed
* organization accounts, for companies and teams
* policy controls, for organizations to coordinate
* single sign-on, for automatic user provisioning
